## The three screens

| file | who | what |

| `screens/nada_merged60_screen.csv` | nada | 62 sequences (22 nada, 10 Saniya, 27 library, parent, 2 decoys), single seed, ipSAE + ipTM + epitope Jaccard |

The three are **not comparable to each other.** That is the main finding, not
a caveat.

## 1. No design is distinguishable from the parent

Dominik's screen is the only one with replicates, so it is the only one that
supports a test. Parent 0.7854 ± 0.0372 (n = 10); best variant TB_030 at
0.8209 ± 0.0371.

Welch's t on the two means: **t = 2.14, p = 0.033.** With 160 variants tested,
the Bonferroni threshold is p < 0.000313. The best p in the screen is 0.018 —
**58x too large.**

**0 of 160 designs are significantly above parent.**

Only 61 of 160 (38%) even exceed the parent's mean. For variants with no real
effect you would expect ~50%, so the library skews slightly *deleterious* —
which is what random perturbation of an optimised binding site should do.

## 2. The metric varies more between implementations than between molecules

The identical 242 aa parent sequence:

| pipeline | ipSAE_min |
|---|---|
| Dominik, Boltz, 10 samples | 0.7854 |
| nada, Boltz, 1 seed | 0.6448 |
| **gap** | **0.1406** |

The best variant effect anywhere in the screen is 0.0326. The gap between two
implementations of the same measurement is **4.3x larger**.

Both used Boltz against HER2 domain IV. The difference comes from sampling,
seeds, construct trimming and version — the ordinary degrees of freedom in
setting up a prediction.

## 3. Rankings do not transfer

Ten of Saniya's designs were scored independently by her pipeline (4 seeds)
and nada's (1 seed):

**Pearson r = −0.322.** Negative. Her `LL_CORE_TKK` scored −0.067 on hers and
+0.039 on nada's; `LL_CORE_SYK` was her best at +0.095 and came out at +0.004.

And within her own four seeds, **6 of 11 designs flip sign** between their
median and their worst seed.

## 4. Decoy calibration

From nada's screen, two deliberately broken controls:

| | ipSAE_min | Δ | ipTM | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| parent | 0.6448 | — | 0.8907 | — |
| Y105A (largest anchor deleted, 79 Å² buried) | 0.5570 | −0.088 | 0.8502 | −0.041 |
| scrambled CDR-H3 | 0.4153 | **−0.230** | 0.8014 | −0.089 |

Two things follow.

**ipSAE is far more sensitive than ipTM here** — scrambling the loop costs 36%
of ipSAE but only 10% of ipTM. Rankings built on ipTM are weaker still.

**The useful range is destruction, not improvement.** ipSAE cleanly separates
a broken interface from an intact one. It does not separate an intact
interface from a slightly better one. Hence: filter, do not rank.
